Section C · Weekly Food Supplies

Food Supply Records — One School Week

Our school canteen receives food supplies during the week. Students buy these items each day. At the end of the week, the manager checks how many items were supplied and how many were sold.

Food is delivered or freshly prepared on different days of the week. Each number below is the total for the whole week, added together from all the deliveries.

These figures count FOOD ITEMS, not money. Do not confuse items with AED!

Items supplied and sold

Units: number of food items
Total number of food items supplied and sold this week
Food Item Total Items Suppliedthis week Total Items Soldthis week

Vocabulary

Supplied
How many food items the canteen received during the week.
Sold
How many food items students bought during the week.
Remaining Stock
How many items were left after sales.

How the numbers are connected

Items Supplied − Items Sold = Items Remaining

Canteen Combo Challenge

You have 15 AED to spend at the school canteen!

Can you create a tasty combo without going over your budget?

This is an optional extension. Try it after your missions, or when you finish early.

Menu prices

Your challenge

  1. Visit the Canteen Menu.
  2. Choose TWO DIFFERENT food items.
  3. Calculate the total cost.
  4. Check whether the total is 15 AED or less.
  5. Calculate how much change you would receive from 15 AED.
  6. Explain why your combination fits the budget.
